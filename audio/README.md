# TECHMINES jukebox music

`arpent-studio.mp3` is a 96-second background edit based on “Arpent” by Kevin MacLeod, available from FreePD under CC0 1.0: https://freepd.com/electronic.php. The source recording was supplied by the site owner; its tempo was first reduced to 90% without changing pitch and its level set to 90% of the original.

This edition keeps the original opening tone and first three seconds of that reference. Over four seconds, it moves into a restrained sound bed that retains the low-frequency foundation and sustained tones while smoothing short notes in the upper register. A very light diffuse tail adds depth without an obvious echo. The overall RMS stays matched to the 90% reference. It fades gently at the end before the opening motif returns on repeat. Encoded as 192 kbps MP3. Audio is opt-in and pauses when the browser tab is hidden.
